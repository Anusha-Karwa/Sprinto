Sprinto · Growth · Outbound Agent
From a list of websites to a booked call — while you stay the decision-maker.

Sprinto helps software companies get security certificates like SOC 2 and ISO 27001. Its Growth Lead used to spend 15–20 minutes per company researching, finding the right person and writing a first email — and quality dropped by the end of the list. This robot does that research in about 30 seconds, shows its evidence for every decision, and never contacts anyone without your approval.
